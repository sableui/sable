# Verdant — art direction / illustration style

Style vocabulary only (no subject matter). Derived from a French fintech marketing-site audit, 2026-07-10.

- **Mood:** warm, editorial, painterly-calm, premium, human, restrained.
- **Texture / material language:** two coexisting registers — (a) soft-focus photographic warmth with a natural film-like grade, golden ambient light, muted earth-toned subjects harmonized to the cream/forest palette; (b) heavily blurred, self-luminous color-field gradients, like out-of-focus stained glass — saturated teal, orange, magenta, blue and green zones melting into each other with smooth diffuse transitions. No grain, halftone, or noise effects; no flat-vector illustration anywhere.
- **Lighting / shading:** naturalistic soft window light in photography; gradients glow from within with no directional lighting. Floating white UI-mockup cards carry a single very soft diffuse drop shadow — the only elevation in the entire system.
- **Line / geometric language:** all imagery cropped into large rounded rectangles (10–16px radius, echoing the UI's own radius ladder). UI-mockup overlays reuse the chrome vocabulary: crisp hairlines, pill badges, small circular status dots. No stroke-based illustration.
- **Recurring effects / signature move:** a solid deep-forest-green panel butted directly against a vivid gradient panel, with white UI cards floating over and bleeding across panel edges.
- **Color in illustration vs. chrome:** the UI chrome is strictly two-tone (forest ink on warm cream, zero accent); illustrations break into the full multicolor gradient spectrum plus warm photographic tones. Color is quarantined inside imagery — the functional teal accent appears only inside product-UI mockups, never in marketing chrome.
