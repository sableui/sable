# Art direction — atelier

Derived from a strictly monochrome, photography-led corporate-portfolio reference. No drawn/vector illustration exists in the source — art direction is entirely photographic + typographic.

- **Mood:** cinematic, editorial, athletic, restrained, premium-corporate.
- **Texture/material language:** full-bleed motion photography with subtle film-like warmth and slight grain; archival imagery rendered strict black-and-white; portraits duotone/monochrome on uniform dark studio grounds.
- **Lighting/shading:** natural golden-hour and low-key night lighting inside photography; the UI itself is completely flat — no shadows, no gradients (at most one soft dark gradient ground behind a product render).
- **Line/geometric language:** thin 1–1.5px hairline rules and line icons (~1.5px stroke, monochrome); large rounded-rectangle media panels (~20px radius) echoing the pill buttons — rounding is the unifying geometry.
- **Recurring effects:** backdrop blur / dark glassmorphism on overlays and nav; gray-to-black scroll-reveal on text; parallax tile scatter; giant type used as a graphic object, overlapping/behind imagery.
- **Color inside imagery vs chrome:** deliberate split — all saturation lives in the photography (warm amber, teal, neon product pops) while UI chrome stays strictly monochrome; monochrome-izing photos (B/W archival, duotone portraits) is itself a hierarchy device.

When composing image-generation prompts: carry the style lines above verbatim; the subject line always comes from the current build's content, never from the original reference's subject matter.
