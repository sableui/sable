## Brand icons

The 23 bundled brand marks come from `reicon-brands` 1.0.2. Sable normalizes them
to a 24-unit grid and removes fixed colors. Copyright 2025 REICON; the complete
MIT notice is included in the package as `third-party/reicon-LICENSE.txt` and
available on this site as the [Reicon license](/licenses/reicon.txt).

The software license does not grant trademark rights. Follow each brand's usage
guidelines when displaying its mark.

## Fonts

Presets declare font stacks; applications load the font files. The featured
presets use Figtree, DM Mono, Instrument Sans, Geist Mono, Outfit, Playfair Display,
Roboto Mono, IBM Plex Mono, Inter, Instrument Serif, Work Sans, Albert Sans,
Unbounded, and Red Hat Mono. The documentation also offers Josefin Slab,
IBM Plex Sans, Martian Mono and Sora in its font picker. These families are
available through [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/) under their respective
font licenses.

Self-hosted repository fonts retain adjacent OFL notices; include those notices
when redistributing the font files.
